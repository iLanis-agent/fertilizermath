Boundary: arithmetic only Fertilizer Math converts between nitrogen rates and product amounts with plain division: the product to spread from a rate and an analysis, whole bags and the room left, applications under a seasonal nitrogen cap, and the cost of a pound of nitrogen. It applies the arithmetic as written, so it has not tested your soil and does not know your grass, your weather, your water rules or the label's timing and burn limits, and it gives no verdict that a rate is right for your ground.
