Fertilizer Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page converts between nitrogen rates and product amounts with plain division: the product to spread from a rate and an analysis, whole bags and the room left, applications under a seasonal nitrogen cap, and the cost of a pound of nitrogen. The rate, the analysis, the area, the bags and the prices are yours to read off your own bag and your own yard. It applies the arithmetic as written, so it has not tested your soil and does not know your grass, your weather, your water rules or the label's timing and burn limits, and it gives no verdict that a rate is right for your ground. Every number here is your own entry.

The product truth

Product to spread: the nitrogen rate divided by the analysis.

The bags truth

Whole bags: rounded up, and the room left in the last bag.

The season truth

Applications under a seasonal nitrogen cap: floor division, nothing rounded up.

The cost truth

The cost of a pound of nitrogen: the bag price over the nitrogen in the bag.
